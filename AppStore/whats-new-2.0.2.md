# JellyGoose 2.0.2 — App Store copy (all three platforms)

A small release on top of 2.0.1, build 109, entered 2026-11-04. It carries what was
merged after 2.0.1 went out: the Shows filter in TV libraries, a show's seasons
listing their episodes in Requests, a season chosen on Home opening its show's
page, and on Apple TV the letters fix in libraries.

The app's What's New card shows 2.0's on 2.0.2 (`WhatsNew.version` maps 2.0.2 to 2.0),
so someone coming straight from 1.8.1 still sees it, and no one sees it twice.

Promotional text, description, keywords, screenshots and review notes: 2.0.1's,
unchanged (App Store Connect carries all but the promotional text to a new version,
so that is copied from 2.0.1; What's New is set).

## iPhone and iPad — What's New

```
• Requests: open a show's season to see its episodes, when each one airs, and which are already in your library or downloaded
• TV libraries: a Shows filter, to list only the shows you choose, or just their episodes
• Fixed: a season in Recently Added on Home opened like a film and couldn't be played; it now opens the show on that season
```

## Apple TV — What's New

```
• Requests: open a show's season to see its episodes, when each one airs, and which are already in your library
• TV libraries: a Shows filter, to list only the shows you choose, or just their episodes
• Fixed: a season in Recently Added on Home opened like a film and couldn't be played; it now opens the show on that season
• Fixed: in a library, pressing left from the letters down the side opened the menu instead of going back to the titles
```
