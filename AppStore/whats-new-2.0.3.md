# JellyGoose 2.0.3 — App Store copy (all three platforms)

A small release on top of 2.0.2, build 110, entered 2026-11-13. It carries what was
merged after 2.0.2 went out: a long press on Home leading to a title's page, song
playlists that opened empty (issue #11) and their move out of Collections, and on
Apple TV the Back fix.

The app's What's New card shows 2.0's on 2.0.3 (`WhatsNew.version` maps 2.0.3 to 2.0),
so someone coming straight from 1.8.1 still sees it, and no one sees it twice.

Promotional text, description, keywords, screenshots and review notes: 2.0.2's,
unchanged (App Store Connect carries all but the promotional text to a new version,
so that is copied from 2.0.2; What's New is set).

## iPhone and iPad — What's New

```
• Home: press and hold the banner, an episode or a programme On Now to go to its page, or its show's page for an episode
• Fixed: song playlists made from playlist files, or on servers that don't say what a playlist holds, opened empty; they now open with their songs
• Song playlists are now kept in Music under Playlists, and Collections keeps to films and shows
```

## Apple TV — What's New

```
• Home: press and hold an episode or a programme On Now to go to its page, or its show's page for an episode
• Fixed: Back from a page opened from another page, like an actor's from a film's, went back two screens instead of one
• Fixed: song playlists made from playlist files, or on servers that don't say what a playlist holds, opened empty; they now open with their songs
• Song playlists are now kept in Music under Playlists, and Collections keeps to films and shows
```

## Mac — What's New

```
• Home: right-click the banner, an episode or a programme On Now to go to its page, or its show's page for an episode
• Fixed: song playlists made from playlist files, or on servers that don't say what a playlist holds, opened empty; they now open with their songs
• Song playlists are now kept in Music under Playlists, and Collections keeps to films and shows
```
