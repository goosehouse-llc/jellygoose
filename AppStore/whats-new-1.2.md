# JellyGoose 1.2 — App Store copy

For the 1.2 version (build 11 or later), Apple TV only. The description and
keywords from 1.1 still hold; see `listing.md`.

## What's New — 1784 / 4000

```
A new Home
• The highlighted title fills the top of the screen with its picture, logo and details
• Rows glide into place a press at a time, and Back takes you to the top row, then the menu
• A lighter menu: it opens over the page instead of pushing it aside, with a row for each library and whoever's watching at the top

Libraries, reworked
• Quicker to move through, a row at a time, with the posters filling the screen
• Sort either way round, and by date played, running time or at random
• Choose several genres, or decades, at once
• Each library remembers how you left it
• Episodes show the day they first aired

Quicker to start
• MKV films now play straight from the file on the Apple TV, so they start at once instead of waiting for your server to convert them, and keep Dolby Vision, HDR and Atmos
• Films with Blu-ray or DVD picture subtitles start just as quickly, with the subtitles drawn where the disc puts them
• Every film is made ready while you're reading its page, so Play goes straight to playing
• Scrubbing previews are fetched while the film plays, so the frame is there wherever you scrub to
• Rather use VLC? Settings, Playback, Video player has a new VLC for MKV option

Better sound
• TrueHD and DTS tracks now reach the Apple TV in full 7.1, lossless, instead of 5.1
• Films played in VLC start on the right language instead of whichever track the file flags

Cast and crew
• Select a face for a short biography: when and where they were born, their age, and their story
• Their films appear once each, however many copies you keep

And more
• A tip jar in Settings, for anyone who'd like to support JellyGoose. Everything stays free
• Smoother throughout: the menu, Home and libraries redraw far less as you move, and the app asks your server for less
```
