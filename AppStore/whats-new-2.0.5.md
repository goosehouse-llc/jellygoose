# JellyGoose 2.0.5 — App Store copy (all three platforms)

A small release on top of 2.0.4, build 112, prepared 2026-11-28. It carries what was
merged after 2.0.4 went out: Requests' posters and pictures, which were missing
everywhere with Seerr 3.5.2 and later where its image caching is off (Seerr then
refuses to hand out pictures, so the app fetches them from The Movie Database, as
Seerr's own site does; #25), and Requests on iPhone with Seerr's own gap between
cards and rows closer together (#24).

The app's What's New card shows 2.0's on 2.0.5 (`WhatsNew.version` maps 2.0.5 to 2.0),
so someone coming straight from 1.8.1 still sees it, and no one sees it twice.

Description: 2.0.4's with the paragraph under Nothing Phones Home changed, as in
`listing.md`, `listing-ios.md` and `listing-mac.md`, since Requests' pictures can now
come from The Movie Database. It was "No accounts, no subscription, no analytics, no
advertising, no third parties. JellyGoose talks to your own servers and nowhere else,
apart from asking your own network where they are during setup." It is now "No
accounts, no subscription, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. JellyGoose
talks to your own servers, never to us."

Promotional text, keywords, screenshots and review notes: 2.0.4's, unchanged
(App Store Connect carries all but the promotional text to a new version, so that
is copied from 2.0.4; What's New and the description are set).
