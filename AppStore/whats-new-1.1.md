## What's New — 1760 / 4000

```
Starts faster
• Films and episodes start sooner: your server gets to work the moment you press Play, while the TV switches picture mode
• A resumed film starts straight from where you left it
• Up Next over the credits starts the next episode after 10 seconds instead of 20

A new player
• A row of buttons along the bottom for subtitles, audio, subtitle timing, speed and chapters
• Chapters marked on the timeline, a preview of where a scrub will land, and the time the film will end
• Smoother scrubbing: hold left or right and it speeds up the longer you hold
• Press down for a card about what's playing
• Resumed films start with the sound in step with the picture, including over a HomePod

Big libraries
• An A to Z down the side of any big library sorted by title. Press right to go into it, or hold to scroll and then click to jump a letter at a time
• Back from a film's page lands on that film, wherever it is in the library

Home
• Continue Watching and Next Up stay up to date wherever you watched from
• Take a show off Next Up from its long-press menu
• Sharper artwork on Home and on every film's page

Watched
• Select several episodes and mark them watched in one go, or mark every episode before this one
• Mark a whole season from its long-press menu
• Watched films and shows carry a tick, and films part-way through show how far

And more
• Pin as many filters as you like: the menu scrolls
• Favourites and Collections join the menu once there's something in them
• Search finds films and shows
• New server and sign-in screens, and an address typed without a port tries 8096
• A show's episodes scroll smoothly, each with its description under the card
• Opening the menu keeps the whole page on screen
• Faster and smoother throughout
```
